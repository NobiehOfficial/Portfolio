Designer, not decorator.

I'm Wokorach Norbert — a graphic designer based in Gulu, Uganda, working with brands that want to be recognized, not just seen. My work sits where strategy meets craft: clear thinking, obsessive typography, and a strong point of view.

I focus on considered visual identities, digital design, and art direction, bringing clarity and care to every project.
